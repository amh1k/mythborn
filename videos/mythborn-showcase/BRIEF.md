## Intent

Create a 90-second product showcase that follows one discovery through the real Mythborn web app. Show the hero atlas, founding a civilization, submitting a photo, the agents' interpretations and rebuttals, the chronicle, and the lasting beliefs, mysteries, and culture. Keep the real app screens central and use text-led callouts. The video should feel like Mythborn, not a generic AI product ad.

## Assets

- A bundled image from the Mythborn project — use as the demo discovery photo.
- The real Mythborn web app, captured locally with demo data; no production account or project data.
- The app's own logo, artwork, fonts, and interface.

## Customizations

- Capture and feature the actual app screens, with local demo responses for populated civilization and debate views.
- Use concise on-screen callouts; no narrated voiceover.
- Carry Mythborn's visual style into the video's titles and transitions, deriving the frame design from the app.
- Review the storyboard and rough layout sketches before building the final frames.
- Latest approved revision: remove editorial caption boxes. Lead each idea with large animated words, carry that movement into the app, and keep the camera travelling between meaningful details. Avoid text-and-screen holds followed by slide changes.
- Polish only: retain the approved kinetic edit, correct cursor targets and travel, wait for capture assets to settle, and frame complete relevant headings and controls. Keep the runtime, typography, and scene order.
- Make the outdoor loop explicit: a walk, a photograph, the agents' interpretations, and an optional suggestion that invites another outing. Use the existing captured suggestion; keep any-photo submission optional rather than implying a location check or required quest.

## Notes

- Focus on the main player journey: atlas, founding, civilization, discovery, debate, chronicle, beliefs, mysteries, and culture. Leave admin and settings out of this 90-second cut.
- Keep the interface readable at 16:9; use measured transitions and animation to guide attention without covering important UI.
