# Mythborn visual direction

Mythborn is the civilization's field journal: visible evidence, competing testimony,
and a saved historical account. The photo or the latest incident supplies identity.
The brand retains illustrated mythology, warm ivory, midnight ink, vermilion, and
four speaker emblems. Routine pages use quiet rules and generous reading space.

## Typography

DM Sans handles body text, controls, and routine headings. Uncial Antiqua is reserved
for the wordmark and founding/sign-in chapter headlines. Almendra supplies selected
civilization names, quotations, and chronicle prose. All fonts are bundled locally
with their licenses. Avoid uppercase manuscript labels repeated before every paragraph.

## Composition

The discovery page is one reading column: submitted photo, factual description,
compact stage tracker, any required decision, debate, chronicle, committed changes.
Never place an introductory sidebar or full roster ahead of available testimony.
Show agent activity without implying that a completed response is still working.

World cards display the actual last chronicle. Do not assign historical meaning to
stock scenery based on an identifier. Landscape artwork belongs on sign-in/founding;
routine pages have no automatic scenery rotation. Proposals remain part of testimony;
only revision records appear in What changed, with before/after state and a reason.
An unresolved verdict is a completed historical outcome, not a failed round.

## States and accessibility

Show saved stage after reload; temporary transcripts may be unavailable after history
retention. Quota waits show the next automatic attempt, while an exhausted budget shows
Retry and Set aside. Observer and God can watch but have no direct message controls.
Messenger conversations remain separate from the four-agent debate.

Support 320 px, large text, visible keyboard focus, native labels, touch targets,
light/dark themes, and reduced motion. Decorative symbols supplement clear labels.
Both themes follow the saved device/user preference. No historical claim is invented
for decoration. Shared tokens/controls live in `src/styles/theme.css`; page rules in
`src/styles/app.css`; episode logic in `src/pages/episode.tsx`.
